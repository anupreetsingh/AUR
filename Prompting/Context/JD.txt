The Opportunity

This is a unique opportunity to join a new engineering team at the ground floor. As an SDE 1, you will learn from senior and staff level engineers while contributing to real production systems that power global trade. We are building Flexport’s client-facing rates platform and a new self-serve booking experience, moving the company from an assisted-sales model to a tech-run one.

You will help build the "front door" of our Client App: the interfaces and services that allow customers to see pricing and book shipments without manual intervention. This is an AI-first team where you will see firsthand how intelligent automation is applied to real-world logistics. If you are looking for a high-growth environment where you can see your code impact thousands of shipments across 112 countries, this is the team for you.

You Will
Design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain systems that represent the real-world intricacies of global logistics and pricing logic.
Work within an AI-integrated codebase, learning how to build and maintain services that support autonomous booking and intelligent quoting.
Contribute to code reviews to maintain code quality, share knowledge, and learn from experienced mentors on the team.
Collaborate with engineering, product, and design to translate complex business problems into elegant technical solutions.
Build highly available, secure, and distributed services within a modern microservices architecture.
Build and launch products rapidly and incrementally: we ship to production dozens of times per day.
Ensure high-quality standards by incorporating metrics and monitoring into every service you touch.
