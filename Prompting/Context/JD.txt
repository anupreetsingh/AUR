Job Overview
Come join Intuit as a Software Engineer 1 on the Future of Discovery & Engagement (FODE) team within QuickBooks.

FODE powers adoption and awareness, upgrades and conversion, and repeat consumption across high-traffic product surfaces. We are building the next-generation contextual discovery and personalization platform that embeds intelligent, real-time recommendations directly into customer workflows.

As an SWE1, you will contribute to building scalable, high-performance web experiences that support experimentation, personalization, and AI-driven discovery. You’ll work closely with senior engineers, product managers, designers, and data scientists to help deliver customer-centric experiences that drive measurable business outcomes.

This role is ideal for engineers early in their careers who are excited to build impactful customer-facing features, learn modern web architectures, and grow within a high-visibility, growth-focused platform team.


Responsibilities

Design, build, and maintain scalable web applications using ReactJS, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.

Implement reusable, modular UI components that support experimentation, personalization, and contextual discovery.

Build responsive, accessible (WCAG-compliant), and internationalized experiences across browsers and devices.

Integrate frontend applications with backend services (Node.js/Java/Kotlin) powering recommendation engines, experimentation platforms, and real-time targeting systems.

Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code following engineering best practices.

Develop and maintain automated test coverage using Cypress or Playwright for end-to-end testing.

Contribute to instrumentation and telemetry to measure customer engagement and feature performance.

Participate in code reviews, sprint planning, and continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) processes.

Troubleshoot, debug, and optimize application performance.

Collaborate effectively with cross-functional partners including product, design, data science, and platform teams.


Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field (or equivalent practical experience).

1+ years of software development experience (internships and co-ops included).

Strong proficiency in JavaScript (ES6+), ReactJS, HTML5, and CSS3.

Experience building responsive and component-based web applications.

Familiarity with RESTful APIs and client-server architecture.

Experience with version control systems such as Git.

Exposure to automated testing frameworks such as Cypress or Playwright.

Understanding of software engineering fundamentals including data structures, algorithms, and object-oriented design.

Strong problem-solving skills and a growth mindset.

Ability to work effectively in a collaborative, fast-paced environment.

Preferred Experience
Experience building or contributing to SaaS or high-traffic web applications.

Familiarity with Node.js or backend service integration.

Experience working with experimentation platforms, feature flags, or A/B testing frameworks.

Exposure to personalization systems or AI-driven user experiences.

Knowledge of performance optimization and frontend observability tools.

Experience contributing to reusable design systems or shared UI libraries.

Why Join FODE?

Work on high-visibility product surfaces that directly impact QuickBooks growth.

Contribute to AI-driven personalization and real-time customer engagement experiences.

Build modern, scalable frontend systems using React and contemporary testing frameworks.

Learn from experienced engineers and grow within a strategic platform team.

See measurable business impact from the features you ship.
