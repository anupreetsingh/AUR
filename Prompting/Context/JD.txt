About the job
⚡ Forward Deployed Engineer

 📍 San Francisco

 💰 $180K+ (DOE) + Equity



We’re partnered with a fast-growing, YC-backed AI fintech startup founded by alumni from MIT, Harvard, and Georgia Tech, including founders with a prior successful exit. The company is already revenue-generating and rapidly scaling its enterprise platform across Fortune 1000 customers.
Their product is transforming accounts receivable operations using AI to automate invoicing, cash application, and collections—helping large organizations accelerate payments, reduce manual workflows, and eliminate revenue leakage.


They’re now looking to hire a Forward Deployed Engineer to work closely with the CTO at the intersection of engineering, product, and enterprise customers, deploying AI solutions directly into complex real-world environments.


This is a highly hands-on role for someone who enjoys ownership, customer interaction, and solving ambiguous technical challenges while shipping impactful solutions quickly.


What You’ll Do
Work directly with the CTO to deploy, customize, and scale AI-driven solutions for enterprise customers
Act as a technical point of contact and client-facing partner, owning implementations from discovery through production rollout
Handle ad-hoc technical challenges including debugging, integrations, data workflows, and custom configurations
Translate customer requirements into scalable, maintainable technical solutions while maintaining product integrity
Collaborate with engineering, product, and customer success teams to influence the product roadmap and platform improvements
Support integrations with enterprise systems such as ERPs, accounting platforms, and internal financial tools
Occasional on-site travel with customers (~10%, optional for more)


What They’re Looking For
Strong software engineering background with experience building or deploying production systems
Comfortable working directly with customers while remaining deeply technical
Thrives in fast-paced, ambiguous startup environments and enjoys wearing multiple hats
Experience working with APIs, integrations, and modern backend systems
Strong problem-solving ability and the confidence to troubleshoot complex technical issues
Excellent communication skills with both technical and non-technical stakeholders


Nice to Have
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field
Experience in enterprise SaaS, fintech, or AI-driven platforms
Prior experience in forward deployed engineering, solutions engineering, or technical consulting
Familiarity with financial workflows such as billing, invoicing, or accounting systems
