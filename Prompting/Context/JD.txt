ABOUT GENALYTE

At Genalyte, we’re revolutionizing diagnostic testing to empower a healthier future. Genalyte is a San Diego-based diagnostics company developing the Merlin platform, a novel photonic ring resonator-based system designed to deliver rapid, multiplexed diagnostic results. We believe that timely and accurate health insights shouldn’t be a luxury, but a necessity. We are driven by a vision where advanced diagnostic capabilities are accessible, efficient, and seamlessly integrated into healthcare. Genalyte is more than just a laboratory; we are advocates for health empowerment, dedicated to providing insights that can transform lives and communities.

WHY WORK FOR US

Joining Genalyte at this pivotal pre-commercial stage means your work will have real, visible impact — on our technology, our culture, and ultimately on how diagnostic testing is delivered to patients. Regardless of your role, you'll be part of a collaborative team working closely with leadership to build something meaningful from the ground up. Genalyte offers competitive compensation including base salary, significant equity participation, and a comprehensive benefits package. This is a rare opportunity to grow alongside a high-impact company at a genuinely exciting moment in its journey.

POSITION SUMMARY

We are seeking a highly motivated  Software Engineer I to join our dynamic team at a leading biomedical device company. This position will involve designing and developing software systems and tools as part of a team.  Focus will be on developing embedded software to power our Merlin blood-testing product, adhering to medical device standards for software quality.

ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS & RESPONSIBILITIES

These may include but are not limited to: 

Developing software components and participating in integration of software components
Developing software tools to facilitate software test and integration activities
Developing and executing software test protocols and generating test reports
Troubleshooting issues related to system functionality and performance
Contributing to cross-functional teams
TECHNICAL KNOWLEDGE

Familiarity with embedded software system design and concepts
Experience with one or more programming languages such as C/C++/C#/Python, especially .NET
Experience with build systems, unit testing and general best practices in SW development
Proficient knowledge of networking and familiarity with Unix and Windows operating systems
Competent in use of requirements management and issue tracking systems like Jira/Confluence
 
EDUCATION, EXPERIENCE, KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS & ABILITIES

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or similar field
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Excellent communication and teamwork abilities
Ability to author and present technical documents 
Familiarity with Agile, Scrum, and Waterfall methodologies
Experience in life sciences industry is a strong preference
Demonstrated ability to work productively in a team environment
 
PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS & WORK ENVIRONMENT

This is a primarily office-based role in San Diego, CA.
