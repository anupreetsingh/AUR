The Opportunity 

This position works out of our Tech Center in Little Canada, MN in the EP division.
In Abbott’s Electrophysiology (EP) business, we’re advancing the treatment of heart disease through breakthrough medical technologies in atrial fibrillation, allowing people to restore their health and get on with their lives.

As the Software Verification Engineer I, you'll have the chance to, under general supervision, be responsible for designing new products and processes and improving and maintaining existing products. This individual also may execute less complex projects.

What You’ll Work On
May conduct design analysis on components and/or assemblies to assist in the development process by ensuring designs are cost efficient, manufacturable, and reliable.

Communicates with the other engineering personnel to coordinate the interrelated design and assure project completion.

Required Qualifications

Bachelors Degree (± 16 years) with 0-2 years of related experience.
