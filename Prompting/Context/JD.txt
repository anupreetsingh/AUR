MECCA has a large and growing focus in the digital space, and we are looking for a Junior Cloud Engineer to join our Cloud & Platform team. This role is instrumental in supporting and evolving our cloud infrastructure, contributing to a secure, reliable, and scalable AWS environment that powers exceptional digital experiences for our customers and internal teams.

The Role You Could Play:

Reporting to the Cloud Engineering Lead, you will play a hands-on role in supporting MECCA’s cloud infrastructure and operations while developing your skills in cloud engineering within a fast-paced, product-led environment. You will:

Support the deployment, configuration, and maintenance of AWS cloud infrastructure to ensure reliability and scalability

Contribute to Infrastructure as Code (Terraform/OpenTofu), building capability across multi-environment deployments

Participate in cloud operations and incident response, following runbooks and contributing to post-incident reviews

Monitor system health using observability tools (Datadog, CloudWatch) and assist in improving alerting and dashboards

Assist with CI/CD pipelines and automation, troubleshooting issues and writing scripts to streamline operational tasks

Maintain clear and up-to-date documentation, operational procedures, and playbooks to support knowledge sharing and consistency across the team

What You Will Bring:

You are an early-career cloud engineer who is curious, proactive, and eager to learn in a collaborative environment. Ideally, you will bring:

Foundational knowledge of AWS services (EC2, Lambda, S3, VPC, IAM) and cloud networking concepts

Exposure to Infrastructure as Code (Terraform) and version control tools like GitHub

Basic scripting skills (Python, Bash, or PowerShell) and understanding of CI/CD concepts

Familiarity with observability tools and an awareness of SRE principles (SLIs, SLOs, incident management)

Strong communication skills, a service mindset, and a willingness to learn from experienced engineers
