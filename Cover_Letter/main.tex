The full-stack scope — transactional backend, consumer-facing UI, and a low-latency delivery path — maps directly to what Twitch Commerce Engineering ships across its subscription and engagement products. An earlier internship building a consumer-facing MERN-stack application with user authentication and payment flows at Turfco Landscape reinforced the same discipline of owning a complete, user-facing system end to end.

My coursework in Data Structures \& Algorithms and Distributed Systems has given me the foundations this role requires: algorithm reasoning, schema design, and intuition for systems that hold under concurrent load. That background, combined with production experience across full-stack layers and hands-on AWS usage, positions me to ramp quickly and contribute to Twitch's commerce products. I look forward to collaborating cross-functionally, owning full-stack features end to end, and helping ship the transactional and engagement systems that power the Twitch creator economy.
